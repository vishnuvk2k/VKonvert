Questions

Why do my iPhone photos end in .HEIC?

HEIC is Apple’s space-saving photo format. It’s great on iPhone, but many sites and apps only accept JPG.

Does converting lose quality?

At quality 85 or higher the difference isn’t visible. The photo’s rotation is kept.

Is it safe for private photos?

Yes. The photos are converted on your own device and never sent anywhere.
