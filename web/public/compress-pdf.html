Questions

How much smaller will it get?

Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs often shrink by 50–90%. PDFs that are mostly text are already small and change less.

Does text get blurry?

No. Only the pictures inside are recompressed; text and drawings stay as they are.

What if it can’t shrink my PDF?

You get your original back rather than a bigger file.
