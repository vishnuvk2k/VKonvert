Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. VKonvert does all the work inside your browser or the Windows app. You can even turn off Wi-Fi after the page loads.

How do I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

Drop the photos on the converter above, keep JPG selected and press Convert. You can do dozens at once.

How small can a PDF get?

Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs often shrink by 50–90%. PDFs that are mostly text are already small and change less.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark and no limits. The code is public under the MIT license.

Is there an app?

Yes, a free Windows app with the same tools plus a right-click menu. A Mac version is planned.
