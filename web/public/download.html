VKonvert for Windows

All eleven tools on your PC, plus the right-click menu, saving as HEIC, AVIF and TIFF, PDF passwords, and light and dark mode. Free, works offline.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

That message appears for apps that aren’t code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. VKonvert is open source: you can read every line on GitHub.

Requirements

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. If Windows says the Visual C++ Runtime is missing, VKonvert offers Microsoft’s free download.

Mac

A Mac version is planned. Until then, every tool except PDF passwords works on this website in Safari or Chrome.
