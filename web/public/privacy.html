Privacy

Short version: your files never leave your device, and VKonvert doesn’t collect anything about you.

Your files

On this website, every tool runs inside your browser. The files you add are read and processed on your device and are never sent to a server. The Windows app works the same way, offline.

What this website stores

Only your light or dark mode choice, saved in your own browser. The site can also save itself in your browser so it opens without an internet connection. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

Hosting and fonts

The website is hosted on Vercel, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website host, they see standard request details such as your IP address when your browser loads the pages. They never see your files.
